"Art"

This is a strange concept and word for me. I grew up with it all 
around me, at least the classical form of it: my parents were
quick sketch artists and painters both. Watching them gained me a most natural ability of copying images photorealistically, sketching and capturing whatever I wanted. 
Our house was also a strange place for others - it was messy and the walls seemed often to form no 90 degree angle. Colours, collected things, started projects, instruments, documents and dishes all on top of each other next to the printer on the dining table. If not there, I was also often with my parents on festivals, always surrounded with blinking lights and 5 songs at the same time, everywhere prizes to be won, autotuned voices telling you about fun and screams of people falling from the sky. I love my childhood environment and my present strangeness surely benefitted most from it.

But.. I can't do art. 
I am not an artist. 
I don't know how to do art. 

I say that sitting on a giant pile of sketches, 15 completed or broken off sketchbooks, paintings that hang on walls that don't only belong to me or my family or my friends. 
I say that while in my apartment more than half of a whole room has a wall completely covered with spraypaint and decoration and paint and weird faces and strange patterns. 

What is art really? 
Without looking it up, I just defined it as doing something without giving two fucks: the first for not caring how it turns out, the second for not caring about not caring and in the end caring, but loving, valueing, free of judgement. 

That is at least how I perceive it: care for it when you start, and you will rid yourself of the free output, of flowing uncontrolled and just in easy creation. To put aside the ego completely. 
But don't care for it when you ended, and it will be just 'somethin' you created somewhere somewhen and its meaning is unknown, but you certainly did not give it any.
Other people can always look at what I made and call it art, I can always look at nature and call it art, but giving something this meaning makes the observer part of the artist more than it makes me as the crafter when I spared my second fuck. 

And some of the things I created seem to try to tell me something, but in my performant stance I shall not listen, and some of the things I really glue my ear onto, yet they never even whisper in the slightest as they are just the 583th eye I have produced in the corner of my notes mindlessly, and some of my art I could not overhear, because the tune it sings is so silently loud, creaking always grotesque and it hurts to be present with it. 

The last type of my art may make you think I should lean into that more, or not, anyways I do as I have no other choice than output in the end when I burst over surely. But it is not something supposed to hang on walls always, just as the shadows at night only want to be seen when it is dark, and neither my strange creation nor I want to disturb that peaceful harmony. 

And in the end words are questionable spending again, since the solution presented itself weeks before I typed the first sentence of this post. But it is probably not the hardest thing always of solving something, but coming to terms with the change it brings. 

I am afraid of who I will be when I let myself be free.
And that is my truth of which nobody should ever comfort me.
